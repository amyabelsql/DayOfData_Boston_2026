# On-call guide

1. Confirm the affected service, database, and environment.
2. Record when the issue began and who reported it.
3. Check alerts, recent deployments, job history, and database health.
4. Protect customer data. Do not paste secrets or private data into an issue.
5. Open an issue and record evidence, actions, and decisions.
6. Escalate when the recovery plan or service target is at risk.
7. Hand off the current status, next action, and owner.
8. After recovery, record the cause and follow-up work.

Do not run unreviewed scripts in production during an incident.
