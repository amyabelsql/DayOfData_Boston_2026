# Backup and restore checklist

- Check that full, differential, and log backups meet the recovery plan.
- Confirm recent backup results and alert on missed jobs.
- Keep backup copies protected and separate from the database host.
- Check retention and available storage.
- Restore a backup to a non-production environment on a schedule.
- Run integrity checks after restore.
- Record restore duration and any errors.
- Update the recovery steps after changes to the database or backup jobs.

A successful backup job does not prove that a restore will work.
